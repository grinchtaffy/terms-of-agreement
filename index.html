Using Grinchtaffy LiveChat

Welcome to Grinchtaffy LiveChat. These terms describe the basic rules for using the website, its messaging features, and optional connections.

By using LiveChat, you agree to follow these terms and applicable laws. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

Accounts and eligibility

You are responsible for maintaining your account's security, protecting your sign-in credentials, and the activity performed through your account. Do not share someone else's password or sign in to an account without authorization.

You must be permitted to use this type of service under applicable age and consent laws. Connected services (including Google/Gmail) may have their own age requirements.

Community rules

  • Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, bully, spam or deliberately disrupt other people.
  • Do not upload illegal content, invade privacy, share another person's credentials or distribute malicious code.
  • Do not exploit bugs, bypass access controls, evade bans, or misuse administrative or third-party services.
  • Do not record or distribute another person's calls or private information without the permissions required by law and the service.
  • Respect other users' rights and the rules for servers and group chats.

Moderators or authorized administrators may restrict features, remove content, suspend or ban accounts, and address reports or safety issues, consistent with applicable laws.

Messages and content

You remain responsible for messages, files, photos and other materials you submit. You should only upload content you have the right to share. Other users may see or save content you send to them.

You grant the service a limited permission to host, transmit, display and process the content as needed to provide the features you use and maintain safety and security. This is not a transfer of ownership.

Optional features and integrations

LiveChat may offer calls, AI tools, games, GradeView, YouTube, Gmail and other optional tools. Feature availability and usage limits may change, and some integrations require separate third-party sign-in or user permissions.

Messages, email actions, file uploads and other commands can have real effects. When you move or permanently delete an email through the Gmail integration, for example, that action can change the corresponding Gmail account. Some actions may not be reversible.

Hypothetical school-grade calculations and AI-generated output are informational tools, not guaranteed accurate or official records.

Third-party services

Connected services such as Google, StudentVUE, Cloudflare and other providers have their own rules and availability. LiveChat is an independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by those services merely because it offers an integration.

Moderation, support and access

Reports and relevant records may be reviewed by authorized personnel to enforce these terms and investigate abuse. Administrative support tools may allow limited account-related actions where permitted; that does not authorize obtaining another person's password or misusing connected accounts.

If you think a moderation action was made in error, contact the site operator.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the operator is not responsible for losses caused by outages, third-party services, users' own content or unauthorized access beyond what the law requires. Nothing in these terms removes rights that cannot legally be waived.

Privacy Policy

Overview

Grinchtaffy LiveChat is a messaging website with optional features such as group conversations, calls, AI assistance, games, GradeView and connected Gmail. This policy explains the information involved in those features, how it is used and the choices available to you.

This policy covers the public website at grinchtaffy.github.io and the LiveChat application at /livechat/. Some optional tools are provided by third parties with their own privacy policies.

Information we process

  • Account and profile information: username, account identifier, optional profile photo, bio, status, preferences and linked account information. Password sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication; original passwords should not be placed in the LiveChat database.
  • Messages and social activity: messages, attachments, reactions, friends, group memberships, reports, moderation records and related timestamps.
  • Calls: participants, connection/signaling information, and audio/video transmitted during calls. A recording feature, if enabled, may process recordings with the applicable controls and required consent.
  • Technical information: browser/device details, timestamps, connection status, error or abuse-prevention information and IP/network information collected by hosting and service providers.
  • Feedback: answers to voluntary surveys, ratings, usernames and submission times when users send feedback.
  • Optional connected apps: information you authorize through Gmail, contacts, StudentVUE GradeView and other integrations, as further explained below.

Why we use information

We process information to operate accounts, deliver messages and calls, enable features users select, show presence and notifications, troubleshoot errors, respond to requests, secure accounts, enforce rules and maintain the service.

Messages and app content may be available to recipients, group members or authorized moderation personnel as appropriate for the feature. Do not assume chats are end-to-end encrypted.

Google / Gmail information

If you choose to connect a Google account to the optional Mail integration, LiveChat requests authorization through Google's sign-in process. Depending on the permissions you approve, LiveChat may access the selected account's email address, email metadata and content, labels, drafts, attachments and contact details needed for the Mail experience.

Requested actions can include listing and reading email, searching, sending and drafting messages, marking read or unread, starring, archiving, applying labels, moving messages to Trash or Spam, restoring them and, only if separately authorized, permanent deletion. These changes affect the linked Google account.

Formatted HTML emails and externally hosted images may be displayed automatically when you open a message. Loading remote images can tell email senders or their providers that a message was opened and may reveal network information. You can disable external images from the message reader; images are rendered inside a restricted viewer.

Google OAuth credentials and account tokens are handled by backend services rather than being intentionally exposed as readable passwords in the site. Connected account data is used to display or perform the Mail features you request. We do not use Google user data for advertising targeting, sell it, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Access by human operators is limited to users' express permission or cases permitted by Google's policies, such as security or legal compliance.

Google API Limited Use: Grinchtaffy LiveChat's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect a Google account in the app where that option is available and revoke access in your Google Account permissions. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not undo actions already made in Gmail.

GradeView and school information

GradeView is an optional integration for displaying information retrieved from StudentVUE, such as grades, assignments, attendance and hypothetical grade calculations. A StudentVUE sign-in may create a saved session so you do not need to re-enter credentials on every visit. School authentication can expire or be revoked independently of LiveChat.

Hypothetical changes are estimates and do not change official school records. If optional grade-sharing features are offered, the account holder should be clearly informed about what is shared and with whom before enabling them. Do not enter school passwords into public chats.

AI and optional tools

If you use the AI Assistant, prompts and files you submit may be sent to configured AI service providers to generate responses. AI output can contain mistakes; avoid entering passwords, private tokens or sensitive personal information into prompts.

Other optional connections such as YouTube, remote browsing and games may process additional information required to provide those features. Each third-party service can have its own terms and privacy policy.

Service providers and sharing

LiveChat relies on technical providers including GitHub Pages for website hosting, Firebase/Google for accounts and database features, and Cloudflare for backend services and optional website analytics. Authorized AI providers, Google APIs and other connected services may process data only when relevant features are used.

Information may also be shared with other users as part of messages and groups; for moderation and security where necessary; or in response to a valid legal requirement. We do not promise that messages, attachments, or public profile information are invisible to authorized administrators.

Analytics and browser storage

The public homepage and LiveChat may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand visits and performance. Analytics and hosting providers can receive information such as page URLs, approximate location, browser details and referral sources.

Browser storage and cookies or similar technologies may be used for sign-in sessions, UI preferences, optional integrations and related functionality. Your browser offers controls for site data and permissions, but disabling some storage may affect sign-in or other features.

Retention and security

Account, message, moderation and connected-app information is kept for as long as reasonably needed to operate the relevant features, respond to support and safety issues, or satisfy legal obligations. Some data may remain for a limited period in caches, backups, provider systems or records required for security.

We use available access restrictions and security features, but no online service can guarantee perfect security. Do not reuse important passwords or share account credentials.

Your choices and requests

You can generally change available account and notification preferences, disconnect optional integrations and leave conversations through LiveChat's settings. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account using the contact information below. Whether a particular request can be fulfilled depends on applicable law, account verification, technical limitations and legitimate retention needs.

If you wish to delete your LiveChat account or have concerns about data associated with it, contact the operator below. Disconnecting Gmail does not delete the emails inside your Google account.

Children and age requirements

Users must meet the minimum age requirements of applicable law and the connected services they use. Where a parent or guardian's consent is legally required for personal information, the required permission must be obtained before the child uses the relevant features. If you believe personal information from a child was collected without required permission, contact us for review and deletion.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published. Material changes should be communicated where required by law.
